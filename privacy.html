MathEdu Visual Mathematics Platform ("API Client") uses YouTube API Services (including YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Embedded Players) solely to publish our own original step-by-step visual mathematics proof lectures to our official YouTube channel (@min7014_ai) and to embed those public educational videos on https://min7014.github.io/mathedu/video_board.html for free student and teacher learning.

3. Data Collection, Storage, and Deletion Policy

  • Zero External User OAuth Collection: Our API Client only authenticates our own internal official channel administrator account (@min7014_ai) for automated educational lecture publishing. We do not request, collect, store, or share OAuth tokens or personal YouTube account data from external website visitors, students, or teachers.
  • Data Deletion & Refresh Policy: Public video metadata (video IDs, titles, and URLs of our own channel uploads) is refreshed within 24 hours and immediately deleted upon video removal. Users may request deletion of any stored inquiry data at any time via our contact email below, and all requests are processed within 72 hours.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Our web learning platform uses standard browser localStorage solely to save student quiz progress locally on the user's device. Embedded YouTube players may place cookies governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Revoking YouTube API Access (Google Security Settings)

Any authorized administrator or user who has granted OAuth permissions to our API Client can revoke our API Client's access to their data at any time via the official Google Security Settings page:

🔗 Revoke Access: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (or https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
