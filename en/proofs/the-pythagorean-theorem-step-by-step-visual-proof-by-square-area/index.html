The Pythagorean Theorem — Step-by-Step Visual Proof by Square Area Partition

25-step visual proof · Geometry · Euclidean Plane Geometry

Original step-by-step proof (PDF) Construct it in MathLab Split-screen study board Source page & applets

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. Step 1

    In this step-by-step visual lecture, we prove the Pythagorean Theorem, c squared equals a squared plus b squared, by partitioning the area of a square.

  2. Step 2

    Starting on a blank board, we begin by constructing a single right triangle.

  3. Step 4

    We mark the interior angle at vertex C as a ninety-degree right angle.

  4. Step 8

    Now we extend the vertical side CA upward by length a to point D, so the total vertical length CD becomes a plus b.

  5. Step 9

    Similarly, we extend the horizontal base CB to the left by length b to point F, making the total horizontal length CF also equal to a plus b.

  6. Step 10

    We now complete the large outer square C D E F of side length a plus b, marking the right angles at its corners.

  7. Step 11

    On the top side DE, we mark point G so that segment EG has length a and segment GD has length b.

  8. Step 12

    On the left side EF, we mark point H so that segment EH has length b and segment HF has length a.

  9. Step 13

    Connecting points A, G, H, and B with straight segments partitions the large square into four corner right triangles surrounding an inner quadrilateral AGHB.

  10. Step 14

    All four corner right triangles share legs of lengths a and b with an included ninety-degree angle, so they are congruent by Side-Angle-Side congruence.

  11. Step 15

    Because the two acute angles of each right triangle sum to ninety degrees, subtracting them from a straight 180-degree angle proves that every interior angle of quadrilateral AGHB is ninety degrees.

  12. Step 16

    With all four sides equal to hypotenuse c and all four angles equal to ninety degrees, the inner figure AGHB is a true square of side length c.

  13. Step 18

    Since all four corner right triangles are congruent, their combined area is 4 times one-half a b.

  14. Step 19

    Meanwhile, we mark the area of the inner tilted square AGHB of side length c in red as c squared.

  15. Step 20

    Adding the four corner triangles and the central square gives the sum of the pieces: 4 times one-half a b, plus c squared.

  16. Step 21

    Next, we set this sum of the five interior pieces equal to the area of the entire outer square.

  17. Step 22

    Since the outer square CDEF has side length a plus b, its total area on the right-hand side is the quantity a plus b squared.

  18. Step 23

    Simplifying the left side gives 2 a b plus c squared, while expanding the right side yields a squared plus 2 a b plus b squared.

  19. Step 24

    Subtracting the common area 2 a b from both sides leaves c squared equals a squared plus b squared, completing our visual proof of the Pythagorean Theorem!
