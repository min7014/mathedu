    Since the linear transformation always preserves the parallelism of straight lines and the equally spaced lattice, the first and second column vectors of the matrix, which are the arrival points of the two basis vectors, soon become the two neighboring sides of the transformed parallelogram, and this is the stage in which the principle of performing the horizontal shear transformation (Cavalieri equivalent transformation), which creates a parallelogram by pushing the upper side of the shape diagonally in the horizontal direction while maintaining the vertical height, is completed. The conclusion that the geometric transformation law that performs the isostatic transformation holds is finally proven!
