Formal proof verification (Lean 4)

✔ Machine-checked in Lean 4 (0 sorry)

From perpendicular bisector equidistance dOA = dOB and dOB = dOC, formally prove concurrency equidistance dOA = dOC to all three vertices.

View Lean 4 theorem source (circumcenter_perpendicular_bisectors_concurrency)
-- 6. [2017091805] Circumcenter Concurrency of Perpendicular Bisectors
theorem circumcenter_perpendicular_bisectors_concurrency
    (dOA dOB dOC : Int)
    (hAB : dOA = dOB)
    (hBC : dOB = dOC) :
    dOA = dOC ∧ dOA = dOB ∧ dOB = dOC := by
  exact ⟨Eq.trans hAB hBC, hAB, hBC⟩
