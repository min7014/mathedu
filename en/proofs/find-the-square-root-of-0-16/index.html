Find the square root of 0.16

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. Steps 6–8

    Now, we apply the idea of converting the decimal number 0.16, which is the key to the proof, into the fraction 16/100, or the square of (0.4) and the square of (-0.4), and confirming it as a symmetrical pair of positive and negative numbers. After this conversion, the structure of an expression that seemed complex can be clearly solved using basic operations that we already know.

  2. Steps 9–12

    (+0.4) times (+0.4) = 0.16, and even when multiplying negative numbers, (-0.4) times (-0.4) = +0.16 holds true, so the equation is derived flawlessly according to the principle that the square roots of both positive and negative numbers exist symmetrically. One thing to be especially careful about at this stage is that it is common to make the mistake of answering the square root of 0.16 as 0.04! When 0.04 is squared, it becomes 0.0016, and the number when squared is 0.16, which is plus or minus 0.4.

  3. Steps 14–15

    Look at the relationship between 5 and 0.16 shown on the screen. If we combine the formulas organized in the previous step into one, we can naturally conclude that the square root of 0.16, which we were aiming for, is the positive square root of +0.4 and the negative square root of -0.4 (plus or minus 0.4)!

  4. Step 16

    The visual proof is complete!
