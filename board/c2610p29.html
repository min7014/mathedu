📐 [2. Complete Whiteboard Solution & Geometric Explanation]

■ Step 1: Condition (가) $f(x) \le f(4) \implies m_1 = 4$
Geometrically, $f(x) \le f(4)$ for all real $x$ means the bell curve $y = f(x)$ reaches its highest peak at $x = 4$. Since the peak of a normal curve is its symmetry axis (mean), we have $m_1 = 4$.

■ Step 2: Condition (나) $f(5) = g(15) \implies \text{Case 1 }(m_2 = 16)$ vs. $\text{Case 2 }(m_2 = 14)$
On curve $f$, $x = 5$ is $1$ unit to the right of the peak $m_1 = 4$. Since curve $g$ is congruent to $f$, a point with the same height $g(15) = f(5)$ must lie at distance $1$ from the peak $m_2$ of $g$, giving two symmetric cases:
• $\text{Case 1}$ ($15$ is $1$ unit left of $m_2$): $m_2 - 1 = 15 \implies \underline{m_2 = 16}$
• $\text{Case 2}$ ($15$ is $1$ unit right of $m_2$): $m_2 + 1 = 15 \implies \underline{m_2 = 14}$

■ Step 3: Standardizing the LHS & Geometric Meaning of Canceling $\cancel{0.118}$
Standardizing $\mathrm{P}(1 \le X \le 2) + \mathrm{P}(7 \le X \le 9)$ with $Z_1 = \dfrac{X - 4}{10}$:
$$\mathrm{P}\left(\frac{-3}{10} \le Z \le \frac{-2}{10}\right) + \mathrm{P}\left(\frac{3}{10} \le Z \le \frac{5}{10}\right) = \mathrm{P}(-0.3 \le Z \le -0.2) + \mathrm{P}(0.3 \le Z \le 0.5)$$ $$= \cancel{0.118} - 0.079 + 0.191 - \cancel{0.118} = \mathbf{0.112}$$ Geometric insight: Reflecting $[1, 2]$ ($Z \in [-0.3, -0.2]$) across the symmetry axis $m_1 = 4$ ($Z = 0$) maps it onto $[6, 7]$ ($Z \in [0.2, 0.3]$), which shares the boundary $X = 7$ ($Z = 0.3$, where $0.118$ cancels) with $[7, 9]$ ($Z \in [0.3, 0.5]$), forming the single contiguous interval $[6, 9]$ ($Z \in [0.2, 0.5]$).

■ Step 4: Testing $\text{Case 1}$ and $\text{Case 2}$ on the RHS $\mathrm{P}(11 \le Y \le 14)$
• $\text{Case 1}$ ($m_2 = 16$): $\mathrm{P}(11 \le Y \le 14) = \mathrm{P}\left(\dfrac{-5}{10} \le Z \le \dfrac{-2}{10}\right) = \mathrm{P}(-0.5 \le Z \le -0.2) = 0.191 - 0.079 = \underline{\underline{0.112}}$ (O.K.! Mirrors $Z \in [0.2, 0.5]$ across $Z = 0$).
• $\text{Case 2}$ ($m_2 = 14$): $\mathrm{P}(11 \le Y \le 14) = \mathrm{P}\left(\dfrac{-3}{10} \le Z \le \dfrac{0}{10}\right) = \mathrm{P}(-0.3 \le Z \le 0) = 0.118 \ne 0.112$ (Invalid! Right endpoint $14$ sits at the peak $Z = 0$).

■ Step 5: Final Tail Probability $\mathrm{P}(Y \ge 20)$
With $m_2 = 16$ confirmed:
$$k = \mathrm{P}(Y \ge 20) = \mathrm{P}\left(Z \ge \frac{20 - 16}{10}\right) = \mathrm{P}(Z \ge 0.4) = 0.5 - 0.155 = 0.345$$
