1단계: 점 $\mathrm{P}, \mathrm{Q}$의 위치 관계 파악
$5\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}} = 2\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BA}} + 3\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}} \implies \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}} = \dfrac{2\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BA}} + 3\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}}}{3 + 2}$이므로 점 $\mathrm{P}$는 선분 $\mathrm{AC}$를 $3 : 2$로 내분하는 점입니다 ($\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} = \dfrac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}, \overrightarrow{\mathrm{CP}} = -\dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}$).
또한 직선 $\mathrm{BP}$ 위의 점 $\mathrm{Q}$에 대하여 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PQ}} = k\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}$라 하면:
$$\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PQ}} = \overrightarrow{\mathrm{CP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PB}} \implies k(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}) = \left(-\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\right)\cdot(-\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}) = \frac{2}{3}(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}})$$ $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}} > 0$이므로 $k = \dfrac{2}{3}$, 즉 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PQ}} = \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}$입니다.

2단계: 삼각형의 넓이 비로 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} = 1$ 도출
$$\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABC}) = \frac{5}{3}\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABP}) = \frac{5}{3} \times \frac{3}{2}\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{APQ}) = \frac{5}{2} \times \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5} = 2\sqrt{5}$$ $$\frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 3 \times \sin A = 2\sqrt{5} \implies \sin A = \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{9} \implies \cos A = \frac{1}{9} \quad (\because A\text{는 예각})$$ 따라서 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} = 3 \times 3 \times \dfrac{1}{9} = 1$입니다.

3단계: 시점을 $\mathrm{A}$로 통일하여 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}}$ 계산
$$\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} = \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} + \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}} = \frac{5}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} - \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} = \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}$$ $$\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}} = \left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}\right) \cdot (\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}) = |\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}|^2 - \frac{5}{3}(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}) + \frac{2}{3}|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}|^2 = 9 - \frac{5}{3} + 6 = \frac{40}{3}$$

✨ [정답 도출 및 실전 점검 포인트]

따라서 $p = 3, q = 40$이므로 $p + q = 3 + 40 = \mathbf{43}$입니다.

📋 [Problem Analysis & Core Concepts]

This problem uses internal division position vectors and dot product identities to relate triangle areas and evaluate $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}}$.

📐 [Step-by-Step Rigorous Derivation]

Step 1: Locate $\mathrm{P}$ and $\mathrm{Q}$
From $5\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}} = 2\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BA}} + 3\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}}$, $\mathrm{P}$ divides $\mathrm{AC}$ internally in $3:2$, and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PQ}} = \overrightarrow{\mathrm{CP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PB}}$ with $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}} > 0$ gives $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PQ}} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}$.

Step 2: Find $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} = 1$
$$\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABC}) = \frac{5}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2}\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{APQ}) = 2\sqrt{5} \implies \sin A = \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{9} \implies \cos A = \frac{1}{9}$$ $$\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} = 3 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{9} = 1$$
Step 3: Compute $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}}$
$$\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}} = \left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}\right) \cdot (\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}) = 9 - \frac{5}{3}(1) + \frac{2}{3}(9) = \frac{40}{3}$$

✨ [Final Answer & Practical Tips]

With $p = 3$ and $q = 40$, $p + q = \mathbf{43}$.
