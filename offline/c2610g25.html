점 $\mathrm{A}(1, 2, k)$를 $xy$평면에 대칭이동한 점은 $\mathrm{B}(1, 2, -k)$입니다.
$$\overline{\mathrm{AB}} = 2k = 4 \implies k = 2 \implies \mathrm{A}(1, 2, 2)$$
2단계: 점 $\mathrm{C}$의 좌표와 $\overline{\mathrm{AC}}$ 구하기
점 $\mathrm{A}(1, 2, 2)$를 $x$축에 대칭이동한 점은 $\mathrm{C}(1, -2, -2)$이므로:
$$\overline{\mathrm{AC}} = \sqrt{(1-1)^2 + \{2-(-2)\}^2 + \{2-(-2)\}^2} = \sqrt{0 + 16 + 16} = 4\sqrt{2}$$

📐 [Step-by-Step Rigorous Derivation]

Step 1: Find $k$
Reflecting $\mathrm{A}(1, 2, k)$ across the $xy$-plane gives $\mathrm{B}(1, 2, -k)$, so $\overline{\mathrm{AB}} = 2k = 4 \implies k = 2$.

Step 2: Find $\overline{\mathrm{AC}}$
Reflecting $\mathrm{A}(1, 2, 2)$ across the $x$-axis gives $\mathrm{C}(1, -2, -2)$, so $\overline{\mathrm{AC}} = \sqrt{0^2 + 4^2 + 4^2} = 4\sqrt{2}$.

      • Core Principle: Reflecting across the $x$-axis preserves the $x$-coordinate and negates both $y$ and $z$.
      • Step Verification ($(1, -2, -2)$): $\mathrm{C}(1, -2, -2)$.

      • Core Principle: The coordinate differences are $\Delta x = 0$, $\Delta y = 4$, and $\Delta z = 4$.
