쌍곡선 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{6^2} = 1$ ($a > 0$)의 점근선의 방정식은:

Since $b = \sqrt{36} = 6$ and $a > 0$, the positive-slope asymptote is $y = \frac{6}{a}x = 2x \implies a = 3$.
