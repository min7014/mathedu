2단계: 정적분으로 부피 $V$ 계산
$$V = \int_1^e \frac{\pi}{8}\left\{1 + (\ln x)^2\right\}dx = \frac{\pi}{8}\left[x + x(\ln x)^2 - 2x\ln x + 2x\right]_1^e = \frac{\pi}{8}\left\{(e + e) - (1 + 2)\right\} = \frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$$

      • 핵심 개념 및 원리: $\int_1^e \{1 + (\ln x)^2\}dx = (e - 1) + (e - 2) = 2e - 3$입니다.
      • 단계 확인 정답 ($2e - 3$): $2e - 3$입니다.
  • 💎 5단계 · [최종 종합] 입체도형의 부피 $V$ 확정하기
      • 핵심 개념 및 원리: 앞의 상수 $\frac{\pi}{8}$을 곱하면 부피 $V = \frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$가 완성됩니다.
      • 단계 확인 정답 ($\frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$): $V = \frac{\pi}{8}(2e - 3) = \frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$이므로 정답은 ③입니다.

✨ [정답 도출 및 실전 점검 포인트]

따라서 입체도형의 부피는 $\dfrac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$이므로 정답은 ③번입니다.

📐 [Step-by-Step Rigorous Derivation]

Step 1: Cross-sectional area
$$S(x) = \frac{\pi}{8}\left\{1 + (\ln x)^2\right\}$$
Step 2: Integrate from $1$ to $e$
$$V = \frac{\pi}{8}\int_1^e \left\{1 + (\ln x)^2\right\}dx = \frac{\pi}{8}\left[(e - 1) + (e - 2)\right] = \frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$$

🪜 [Complete Step-by-Step Scaffolding Walkthrough (Steps 1–5)]

Connecting each elementary-grounded scaffolding step from Step 1 to Step 5 completes the entire derivation without any logical gaps:

  • 🔰 Step 1 · [Elementary Circle Area] Area of a semicircle with diameter $D$
      • Core Principle: In elementary geometry, radius $r = \frac{D}{2}$, so the area of a semicircle is $\frac{1}{2}\pi r^2 = \frac{1}{2}\pi\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{\pi}{8}D^2$.
      • Step Verification ($\frac{\pi}{8}\{1 + (\ln x)^2\}$): $S(x) = \frac{\pi}{8}\{1 + (\ln x)^2\}$.
  • 🌿 Step 2 · [Constant Integral] Evaluating $\int_1^e 1\\,dx$
      • Core Principle: $\int_1^e 1\,dx = e - 1$.
      • Step Verification ($e - 1$): $e - 1$.
  • 🌱 Step 3 · [Integration by Parts] Evaluating $\int_1^e (\ln x)^2 dx$
      • Core Principle: Using integration by parts, $\int_1^e (\ln x)^2 dx = \left[x(\ln x)^2 - 2x\ln x + 2x\right]_1^e = e - 2$.
      • Step Verification ($e - 2$): $e - 2$.
  • 🌳 Step 4 · [Summing Integrals] Computing $(e - 1) + (e - 2)$
      • Core Principle: $(e - 1) + (e - 2) = 2e - 3$.
      • Step Verification ($2e - 3$): $2e - 3$.
  • 💎 Step 5 · [Final Synthesis] Final volume $V$
      • Core Principle: Multiply by $\frac{\pi}{8}$ to get $V = \frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$.
      • Step Verification ($\frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$): $\frac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$ (choice ③).

✨ [Final Answer & Practical Tips]

The correct answer is choice ③ ($\dfrac{(2e - 3)\pi}{8}$).
