📐 [단계별 상세 풀이 전개]

1단계: 1회 시행에서 공을 $1$개 또는 $2$개 넣을 확률
주사위를 두 번 던져 나온 눈의 수의 곱이 홀수이려면 두 번 모두 홀수가 나와야 하므로:
$$P(\text{공 } 1\text{개}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}, \quad P(\text{공 } 2\text{개}) = 1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$$
2단계: $4$회 시행 후 공의 개수가 $5$ 이하일 확률
공의 개수가 $5$ 이하인 경우는 총 $4$개($1$개씩 $4$번) 또는 총 $5$개($1$개씩 $3$번, $2$개 $1$번)일 때이므로:
$${}_{4}\mathrm{C}_{4}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^4 + {}_{4}\mathrm{C}_{3}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^3\left(\frac{3}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{256} + \frac{12}{256} = \frac{13}{256}$$

      • 핵심 개념 및 원리: 구하는 확률은 $\frac{13}{256}$입니다.
      • 단계 확인 정답 ($\frac{13}{256}$): $\frac{1}{256} + \frac{12}{256} = \frac{13}{256}$이므로 정답은 ⑤입니다.

✨ [정답 도출 및 실전 점검 포인트]

따라서 구하는 확률은 $\dfrac{13}{256}$이므로 정답은 ⑤번입니다.

📐 [Step-by-Step Rigorous Derivation]

Step 1: Single-trial probabilities
$$P(1\text{ ball}) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}, \quad P(2\text{ balls}) = \frac{3}{4}$$
Step 2: At most $5$ balls in $4$ trials
$$\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^4 + {}_{4}\mathrm{C}_{1}\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^3 = \frac{1 + 12}{256} = \frac{13}{256}$$

🪜 [Complete Step-by-Step Scaffolding Walkthrough (Steps 1–5)]

Connecting each elementary-grounded scaffolding step from Step 1 to Step 5 completes the entire derivation without any logical gaps:

  • 🔰 Step 1 · [Elementary Odd/Even Multiplication] Probability that the product of two dice is odd
      • Core Principle: By elementary odd/even multiplication, a product of two integers is odd if and only if both are odd. Since each die is odd with probability $\frac{1}{2}$, the probability of adding $1$ ball is $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$.
      • Step Verification ($\frac{3}{4}$): $1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$.
  • 🌿 Step 2 · [Sum of Balls] Cases where 4 trials yield at most 5 balls
      • Core Principle: In $4$ trials, adding $1$ or $2$ balls each time gives a total $\le 5$ iff the total is $4$ (four $1$s) or $5$ (three $1$s and one $2$).
      • Step Verification ($\frac{1}{256}$): $\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^4 = \frac{1}{256}$.
  • 🌱 Step 3 · [Independent Trials] Probability that the total is $5$ balls
      • Core Principle: The probability of three $1$s and one $2$ is ${}_{4}\mathrm{C}_{3}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^3\left(\frac{3}{4}\right) = \frac{12}{256}$.
      • Step Verification ($12$): $4 \times 3 = 12$.
  • 🌳 Step 4 · [Adding Numerators] Adding $1 + 12$
      • Core Principle: Add $\frac{1}{256}$ and $\frac{12}{256}$.
      • Step Verification ($13$): $1 + 12 = 13$.
  • 💎 Step 5 · [Final Synthesis] Final probability
      • Core Principle: The final probability is $\frac{13}{256}$.
      • Step Verification ($\frac{13}{256}$): $\frac{13}{256}$ (choice ⑤).

✨ [Final Answer & Practical Tips]

The correct answer is choice ⑤ ($\dfrac{13}{256}$).
