Prerequisite Knowledge: This is $\lim_{\Delta \to 0}\frac{\sin \Delta}{\Delta} = 1$. To match the angles and denominator of $\sin 5x$, multiply the denominator and numerator by $5$.

먼저 알아야 할 지식

$x \to 0$일 때 $5x \to 0$이므로 $\lim_{x \to 0}\frac{\sin 5x}{5x} = 1$ 입니다. 따라서 $1 \times 5 = 5$ 입니다.
Prerequisite Knowledge: When it is $x \to 0$, it is $5x \to 0$, so it is $\lim_{x \to 0}\frac{\sin 5x}{5x} = 1$. Hence $1 \times 5 = 5$.
