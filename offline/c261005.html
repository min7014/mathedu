1단계: 곱의 미분법으로 도함수 $f'(x)$ 구하기
$f(x) = (x+1)(x^2+3x+1)$의 양변을 $x$에 대하여 미분하면:
$$f'(x) = (x+1)'(x^2+3x+1) + (x+1)(x^2+3x+1)'$$
$$f'(x) = 1 \cdot (x^2+3x+1) + (x+1)(2x+3)$$
2단계: $x = 2$ 대입 계산
식을 전개할 필요 없이 바로 $x = 2$를 대입합니다:
$$f'(2) = (2^2 + 3 \times 2 + 1) + (2 + 1)(2 \times 2 + 3) = 11 + 3 \times 7 = 11 + 21 = 32$$

      • 핵심 개념 및 원리: 곱의 미분법에 넣으면 $f'(x) = 1 \cdot (x^2+3x+1) + (x+1)(2x+3)$이 됩니다.
      • 단계 확인 정답 ($21$): $x = 2$를 대입하면 $(2+1) \times (4+3) = 3 \times 7 = 21$입니다.
  • 💎 5단계 · [최종 합산] 앞쪽 값 $11$과 뒤쪽 값 $21$ 더하기
      • 핵심 개념 및 원리: 1단계에서 구한 앞쪽 괄호 값 $11$과 4단계에서 구한 뒤쪽 곱의 값 $21$을 더하면 $f'(2) = 11 + 21 = 32$입니다.
      • 단계 확인 정답 ($32$): $11 + 21 = 32$입니다.

따라서 $f'(2) = 32$이며, 정답은 ④번입니다.

📐 [Step-by-Step Rigorous Derivation]

Step 1: Apply the product rule
$$f'(x) = (x^2+3x+1) + (x+1)(2x+3)$$
Step 2: Substitute $x = 2$
$$f'(2) = (4 + 6 + 1) + (3)(4 + 3) = 11 + 21 = 32$$

🪜 [Complete Step-by-Step Scaffolding Walkthrough (Steps 1–5)]

Connecting each elementary-grounded scaffolding step from Step 1 to Step 5 completes the entire derivation without any logical gaps:

  • 🔰 Step 1 · [Elementary Arithmetic Link] Evaluating the two factors at $x = 2$
      • Core Principle: At $x = 2$, the first factor is $2+1 = 3$ and the second factor is $2^2 + 3(2) + 1 = 4 + 6 + 1 = 11$.
      • Step Verification ($11$): $4 + 6 + 1 = 11$.
  • 🌿 Step 2 · [Symbol Translation] Understanding the Product Rule
      • Core Principle: When differentiating a product $A \times B$, take turns differentiating each factor and add: $(A \times B)' = A' B + A B'$.
      • Step Verification ($g'(x)h(x) + g(x)h'(x)$): By the product rule, $\{g(x)h(x)\}' = g'(x)h(x) + g(x)h'(x)$.
  • 🌱 Step 3 · [Differentiating Factors] Differentiating each factor separately
      • Core Principle: The derivative of $(x+1)$ is $1$, and the derivative of $(x^2+3x+1)$ is $2x + 3$.
      • Step Verification ($7$): Since $h'(x) = 2x + 3$, $h'(2) = 4 + 3 = 7$.
  • 🌳 Step 4 · [Assembling Derivative] Writing the expression for $f'(x)$
      • Core Principle: Combining the pieces gives $f'(x) = 1 \cdot (x^2+3x+1) + (x+1)(2x+3)$.
      • Step Verification ($21$): At $x = 2$, $(2+1)(4+3) = 3 \times 7 = 21$.
  • 💎 Step 5 · [Final Addition] Adding $11$ and $21$ to get $f'(2)$
      • Core Principle: Add the first part $11$ (from Step 1) and the second part $21$ (from Step 4) to get $f'(2) = 11 + 21 = 32$.
      • Step Verification ($32$): $11 + 21 = 32$.

The value of $f'(2)$ is $32$, which corresponds to Option ④.
