      • Core Principle: Since $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{CP}} = -\dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PB}} = -\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}$, the right-hand side becomes $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{CP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PB}} = \dfrac{2}{3}(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}})$.
      • Step Verification ($\dfrac{2}{3}$): $k = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{PQ}} = \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BP}}$.
  • 🌱 Step 3 · [Triangle Area Ratios] Finding $\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABC})$ from $\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{APQ}) = \dfrac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}$
      • Core Principle: Using base ratios, $\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABP}) = \dfrac{3}{2}\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{APQ})$ and $\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABC}) = \dfrac{5}{3}\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABP})$.
      • Step Verification ($2\sqrt{5}$): $\text{Area}(\triangle\mathrm{ABC}) = 2\sqrt{5}$.
  • 🌳 Step 4 · [Finding $\sin A$ and $\cos A$] Computing $\cos A$ from $\overline{\mathrm{AB}} = \overline{\mathrm{AC}} = 3$ and area $2\sqrt{5}$
      • Core Principle: From $\dfrac{1}{2}(3)(3)\sin A = 2\sqrt{5}$, we get $\sin A = \dfrac{4\sqrt{5}}{9}$. Since $\angle A$ is acute, $\cos A = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 A}$.
      • Step Verification ($\dfrac{1}{9}$): $\cos A = \dfrac{1}{9}$.
  • 🏛️ Step 5 · [Base Dot Product] Computing $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}$
      • Core Principle: The dot product is $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} = |\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}||\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}|\cos A$.
      • Step Verification ($1$): $3 \times 3 \times \dfrac{1}{9} = 1$.
  • ⚡ Step 6 · [Unifying Initial Point] Expressing $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}}$ in terms of $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}$
      • Core Principle: Since $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} = \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} + \dfrac{2}{3}(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} - \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}) = \dfrac{5}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} - \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AP}} = \dfrac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}$, we can simplify $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}}$.
      • Step Verification ($\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}$): $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} = \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}$.
  • 🌟 Step 7 · [Expanding Dot Product] Computing $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AQ}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{BC}} = \dfrac{q}{p}$
      • Core Principle: Expanding $\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}\right)\cdot(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}} - \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}})$ gives $|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}|^2 - \dfrac{5}{3}(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}\cdot\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}) + \dfrac{2}{3}|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}}|^2$.
      • Step Verification ($\dfrac{40}{3}$): $\dfrac{40}{3}$.
  • 💎 Step 8 · [Final Synthesis] Computing $p + q$
      • Core Principle: From $\dfrac{q}{p} = \dfrac{40}{3}$ with $\gcd(3, 40) = 1$, we have $p = 3$ and $q = 40$.
      • Step Verification ($43$): $3 + 40 = 43$.
