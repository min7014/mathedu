$$\left(2x^2 + \frac{1}{2x}\right)^6\text{의 일반항은 } {}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{r} (2x^2)^{6-r} \left(\frac{1}{2x}\right)^r = {}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{r} 2^{6-2r} x^{12-3r}$$
2단계: $x^6$이 되는 $r$의 값과 계수 계산
$12 - 3r = 6$에서 $r = 2$입니다. 이를 계수에 대입하면:
$${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2} \times 2^{6-4} = 15 \times 4 = 60$$

📐 [Step-by-Step Rigorous Derivation]

Step 1: General term
$${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{r} (2x^2)^{6-r} \left(\frac{1}{2x}\right)^r = {}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{r} 2^{6-2r} x^{12-3r}$$
Step 2: Solve for $r$ and evaluate
Setting $12 - 3r = 6 \implies r = 2$ gives ${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2} \times 2^2 = 15 \times 4 = 60$.

🪜 [Complete Step-by-Step Scaffolding Walkthrough (Steps 1–5)]

Connecting each elementary-grounded scaffolding step from Step 1 to Step 5 completes the entire derivation without any logical gaps:

  • 🔰 Step 1 · [Elementary Multiplication & Subtraction] Solving $12 - 3r = 6$ for $r$
      • Core Principle: Using elementary arithmetic, choosing $x^2$ $(6-r)$ times and $x^{-1}$ $r$ times gives exponent $2(6-r) - r = 12 - 3r$. Set this equal to $6$.
      • Step Verification ($2$): $3r = 6 \implies r = 2$.
  • 🌿 Step 2 · [Combination] Computing ${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2}$
      • Core Principle: ${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2} = \frac{6 \times 5}{2 \times 1} = 15$.
      • Step Verification ($15$): $\frac{30}{2} = 15$.
  • 🌱 Step 3 · [Powers] Computing $2^4 \times \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2$
      • Core Principle: At $r = 2$, the numerical factor is $2^4 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = 16 \times \frac{1}{4} = 4$.
      • Step Verification ($4$): $16 \div 4 = 4$.
  • 🌳 Step 4 · [Combining Factors] Multiplying ${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2}$ by $4$
      • Core Principle: The coefficient of $x^6$ is ${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2} \times 4 = 15 \times 4$.
      • Step Verification ($20$): $5 \times 4 = 20$.
  • 💎 Step 5 · [Final Synthesis] Final coefficient of $x^6$
      • Core Principle: $15 \times 4 = 60$.
      • Step Verification ($60$): ${}_{6}\mathrm{C}_{2} \times 2^4 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = 15 \times 4 = 60$ (choice ③).

✨ [Final Answer & Practical Tips]

The correct answer is choice ③ ($60$).
