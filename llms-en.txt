# MathEdu — Step-by-Step Visual Math Proofs (English)

> Free open educational resource. Each page explains why a theorem holds through a numbered sequence of figures, links the original step-by-step proof PDF, an interactive MathLab construction, and English YouTube lectures where available. Audience: secondary-school students, teachers and adult learners.
